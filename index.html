    BA8421 is the compressed zone/digit code IBM's System/360 stored internally: zone bits B, A mark which letter group a character falls in (A–I, J–R, S–Z), and digit bits 8, 4, 2, 1 give its position within that group, in plain binary. A space is all zeros.
